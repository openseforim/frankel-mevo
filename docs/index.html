Zacharias Frankel, Mevo ha-Yerushalmi

Introduction to the Jerusalem Talmud, with an English translation paragraph by paragraph · מבוא הירושלמי

Zacharias Frankel (1801–1875), chief rabbi of Dresden and from 1854 the first head of the Jewish Theological Seminary at Breslau, wrote Mevo ha-Yerushalmi (Breslau 1870) as the first modern introduction to the Jerusalem Talmud: its origin, redaction and language, and an alphabetical lexicon of the amoraim of the Land of Israel. It is still used for the sages of the Yerushalmi, and it has never been translated.

The Hebrew text

  • Base text: the first edition, Breslau 1870. Scan: University Library Potsdam, Digitales Brandenburg 320753 (Public Domain Mark).
  • OCR: Kraken with the PP-OCRv6 model, line by line, then checked against the scan by eye.

Method

  • Rows. One row is one heading or one paragraph of the print.
  • English. Close to the Hebrew; Frankel's references are kept as he gives them, with the translator's additions in gray brackets.
  • Uncertain renderings have the mark ⚠ and a note at the end of the page.
  • Translator. The translation is by Claude (Anthropic), directed and reviewed by Ezra Brand.

Translations that exist

  • None known.
